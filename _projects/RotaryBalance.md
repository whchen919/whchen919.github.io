A new rotary balance system to characterize the aerodynamic charateristics of an aircraft in a spinning motion was developed at the Aerospace Science and Technology Research Center (ASTRC) of National Cheng Kung University (NCKU). Experiments conducted with the Standard Dynamics Model (SDM), which is a generic fighter aircraft model, showed good agreement with available data extracted from previous studies.
